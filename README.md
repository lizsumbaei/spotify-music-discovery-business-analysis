# Spotify Music Discovery & Personalised Recommendation System — Business Analysis Case Study

> **Case Study Disclaimer:** This is an academic Business Analysis case study based on a hypothetical/proposed solution inspired by Spotify's music discovery context. I am not an employee, representative, or contractor of Spotify, and this project does not represent work completed for Spotify.

## Project Overview

This project presents a Business Analysis case study exploring a personalised music discovery and recommendation system.

The project examines business and user needs associated with music discovery and creator visibility and develops requirements for a proposed solution.

**Project type:** Academic Business Analysis Project
**Focus:** Business Analysis, Requirements Engineering and Stakeholder Analysis

## Business Problem

The analysis identified challenges relating to music discovery for listeners and visibility for music creators.

The proposed solution explores how a personalised recommendation approach could improve the discovery experience while supporting broader business and stakeholder needs.

## Stakeholder Analysis

The project considers a range of stakeholders, including:

* Listeners
* Artists
* Record labels
* Product managers
* Data and Machine Learning engineers
* Customer support
* Privacy stakeholders
* Trust and safety stakeholders

Stakeholder needs and responsibilities were considered when developing the proposed requirements.

## Requirements Analysis

Requirements were developed using several analysis approaches, including:

* Document analysis
* Stakeholder analysis
* Mind mapping
* User-story development
* Requirements mapping

The assessment produced **32 user-story requirements** covering different stakeholder groups and system needs.

## User Stories

User stories were developed to represent the needs of different stakeholders, including listeners, artists, product teams, technical teams, privacy stakeholders, trust and safety, and customer support.

The requirements were structured to connect stakeholder needs with proposed system capabilities.

## Requirements Traceability

A requirements traceability approach was used to map stakeholders to requirements.

This helped demonstrate how individual requirements related back to identified stakeholder needs and supported the overall proposed solution.

## Business Analysis

The project also considered:

* Current business capabilities
* Strategic needs
* Industry benchmarking
* Alternative solution approaches
* Proposed solution recommendations

These activities were used to evaluate the proposed approach from both business and stakeholder perspectives.

The project demonstrates how Business Analysis techniques can be used to move from an identified business problem through stakeholder analysis and requirements development toward a proposed technology solution.
