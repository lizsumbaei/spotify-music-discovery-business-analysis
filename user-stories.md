# User Stories

## Case Study Context

This document presents user stories developed as part of an academic Business Analysis case study exploring a proposed personalised music discovery and recommendation system.

The user stories are based on the needs identified through the case study analysis and are intended to demonstrate how business and user requirements can be translated into clear, user-focused requirements.

## User Stories

### US-01 — Personalised Music Recommendations

**As a listener,**  
I want to receive music recommendations based on my listening preferences,  
**so that** I can discover music that is relevant to my interests.

### US-02 — Discover New Music

**As a listener,**  
I want to discover new artists and songs that match my interests,  
**so that** I can explore music beyond what I already know.

### US-03 — Relevant Recommendations

**As a listener,**  
I want recommendations to reflect my listening behaviour and preferences,  
**so that** the recommendations feel relevant to me.

### US-04 — Artist Visibility

**As an artist,**  
I want my music to be surfaced to listeners who may be interested in my style,  
**so that** I can increase the visibility and potential reach of my work.

### US-05 — Support Music Discovery

**As a music creator,**  
I want the recommendation system to provide opportunities for my music to be discovered by relevant audiences,  
**so that** I can reach listeners who are more likely to engage with my work.

### US-06 — Stakeholder Requirements

**As a product stakeholder,**  
I want the proposed recommendation solution to address both listener and creator needs,  
**so that** the solution provides value across key stakeholder groups.

## Acceptance Criteria

The user stories should support a proposed solution that:

- Provides personalised music recommendations based on user preferences and listening behaviour.
- Supports discovery of new artists and songs.
- Improves the visibility of relevant music creators.
- Addresses the needs of key stakeholder groups.
- Provides clear and traceable requirements for the proposed solution.

## Business Analysis Approach

The user stories demonstrate the application of a user-centred Business Analysis technique by connecting stakeholder needs with proposed system requirements.

These user stories are part of an academic case study and do not represent requirements developed for or on behalf of Spotify.
