# Requirements Analysis

## Business Requirements

The proposed music discovery and recommendation system should:

- Improve personalised music discovery for listeners.
- Support greater visibility and discovery for music creators.
- Provide recommendations based on user preferences and listening behaviour.
- Support business and stakeholder needs identified during the analysis.

## Stakeholder Requirements

Key stakeholders identified include:

- Listeners
- Artists
- Record labels
- Product managers

Each stakeholder group has different needs and expectations from the proposed solution.

## Functional Requirements

The proposed system should:

1. Allow users to discover music through personalised recommendations.
2. Use relevant user preferences and listening behaviour to support recommendations.
3. Provide opportunities for users to discover music from a wider range of creators.
4. Support the business objective of improving music discovery and creator visibility.

## User Stories

### Listener

As a listener, I want personalised music recommendations so that I can discover music that matches my interests.

### Music Creator

As a music creator, I want my music to be discoverable by relevant listeners so that I can increase visibility.

### Product Manager

As a product manager, I want the recommendation experience to meet user and business needs so that the product provides value to its stakeholders.

## Requirements Traceability

The requirements are linked to the identified business problem and stakeholder needs.

| Requirement | Stakeholder | Business Need |
|---|---|---|
| Personalised recommendations | Listeners | Improve music discovery |
| Greater creator visibility | Artists | Increase opportunities for music discovery |
| Relevant recommendation experience | Product managers | Support user engagement and product value |
| Broader music discovery | Record labels | Improve visibility of music content |

## Summary

This analysis translates the identified business and stakeholder needs into functional requirements and user stories for the proposed music discovery and recommendation system.
