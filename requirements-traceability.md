# Requirements Traceability

## Case Study Context

This document presents requirements traceability for an academic Business Analysis case study exploring a proposed personalised music discovery and recommendation system.

The traceability approach links identified business needs to proposed requirements and user stories. This helps demonstrate how the proposed solution addresses stakeholder and user needs.

## Requirements Traceability Matrix

| Requirement ID | Business / User Need | Proposed Requirement | Related User Story |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Listeners need relevant music discovery | The proposed solution should provide personalised music recommendations based on listener preferences and behaviour. | US-01, US-03 |
| BR-02 | Listeners want to discover new music | The proposed solution should support discovery of new artists and songs relevant to listener interests. | US-02 |
| BR-03 | Artists need greater visibility | The proposed solution should support opportunities for relevant artists and their music to be surfaced to interested listeners. | US-04, US-05 |
| BR-04 | Stakeholders need a solution that addresses multiple user groups | The proposed solution should consider the needs of listeners, artists, and product stakeholders. | US-06 |
| BR-05 | Requirements need to be clear and traceable | Business and user needs should be documented and linked to proposed solution requirements. | US-06 |

## Traceability Approach

The matrix provides a link between the identified business and user needs and the proposed requirements.

This approach helps ensure that each requirement can be traced back to an identified need and related user story.

## Business Analysis Value

Requirements traceability supports:

- Clear documentation of business and user needs.
- Alignment between requirements and stakeholder expectations.
- Visibility of how user stories relate to proposed requirements.
- Identification of requirements that may require further analysis or validation.
- A structured approach to managing requirements throughout a proposed solution.
