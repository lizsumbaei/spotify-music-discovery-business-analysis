# Business Process Analysis — Spotify Music Discovery & Personalised Recommendation System

> **Case Study Context:** This document forms part of an academic Business Analysis case study. The process described below represents a hypothetical/proposed solution inspired by Spotify's music discovery context. It does not describe Spotify's internal processes or work completed for Spotify.

## 1. Process Overview

The proposed solution focuses on improving the music discovery experience for listeners while also supporting greater visibility and discoverability for music creators.

The process considers how a listener may discover music, interact with recommendations, provide implicit or explicit feedback, and receive more relevant recommendations over time.

The analysis is based on a hypothetical business scenario and is intended to demonstrate Business Analysis techniques including process analysis, requirements identification, stakeholder analysis and requirements traceability.

## 2. Business Problem

The case study identifies two related challenges:

* Listeners may find it difficult to discover music that matches their individual interests.
* Music creators may face challenges gaining visibility and reaching relevant audiences.

A proposed personalised recommendation approach could help connect listeners with relevant music while creating opportunities for greater creator visibility.

## 3. Proposed Future-State Process

The proposed process can be represented as follows:

1. **Listener accesses the music discovery experience**
2. **Listener searches, browses or interacts with available music**
3. **System captures relevant user interactions and preferences**
4. **Recommendation logic identifies potentially relevant music**
5. **System presents personalised recommendations**
6. **Listener interacts with recommended content**
7. **Listener feedback and interaction data contribute to future recommendations**
8. **Recommendations are refined over time**
9. **Relevant music creators may receive increased visibility through discovery**

## 4. Process Participants

The proposed process involves several stakeholders:

| Stakeholder                 | Role in the Process                                                           |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Listener                    | Searches for, discovers and interacts with music                              |
| Music Creator               | Creates music and seeks visibility among relevant audiences                   |
| Product/Business Team       | Defines business objectives and evaluates the proposed solution               |
| Business Analyst            | Analyses stakeholder needs, processes and requirements                        |
| Recommendation/Product Team | Considers how recommendation functionality could support the identified needs |
| Data/Analytics Stakeholders | Analyse relevant interaction and performance information                      |

## 5. Current-State Problem

The case study begins with a problem-focused view rather than assuming that a particular technical solution already exists.

The key business questions are:

* How can listeners discover music that is more relevant to their interests?
* How can the discovery experience reduce the effort required to find suitable music?
* How can music creators improve visibility among potentially interested listeners?
* What business and user requirements would be necessary for a proposed recommendation solution?
* How can stakeholder needs be translated into clear and testable requirements?

## 6. Proposed Future-State Process

The proposed future state introduces a more personalised discovery journey.

### Step 1 — User Discovery

The listener enters the music discovery experience and searches, browses or interacts with available content.

### Step 2 — Interaction and Preference Signals

The proposed solution considers relevant interaction signals, such as the listener's engagement with music.

These signals would be analysed within the scope of the hypothetical solution to support more relevant recommendations.

### Step 3 — Recommendation

The proposed system uses available information to identify music that may be relevant to the listener.

### Step 4 — Personalised Results

Recommendations are presented to the listener as part of the music discovery experience.

### Step 5 — Continued Interaction

The listener can interact with recommended content. These interactions may provide additional information that can contribute to future recommendation refinement.

### Step 6 — Ongoing Improvement

The proposed process is iterative. As the listener continues interacting with music, the recommendation experience can potentially become more aligned with their interests.

## 7. Business Analysis Perspective

From a Business Analysis perspective, the process demonstrates the relationship between:

**Business Problem → Stakeholder Needs → Requirements → Proposed Solution → User Interaction → Feedback → Continuous Improvement**

The process analysis provides a foundation for defining functional and non-functional requirements.

It also supports the development of user stories and acceptance criteria by identifying the activities and outcomes that matter to the stakeholders.

## 8. Key Business Outcomes

The proposed solution aims to support the following outcomes:

* Improved music discovery for listeners
* More relevant personalised recommendations
* Reduced effort involved in discovering suitable music
* Increased opportunities for music creators to gain visibility
* Better alignment between user needs and proposed product functionality
* A clear basis for defining and tracing business and functional requirements

## 9. Process Assumptions

This case study makes the following assumptions:

* The proposed recommendation functionality is hypothetical.
* The solution would operate within appropriate privacy, security and data-governance requirements.
* Relevant interaction information would be available for analysis.
* Recommendation results would be evaluated against defined business and user requirements.
* The proposed process is intended for Business Analysis demonstration purposes rather than as a description of Spotify's actual internal processes.

## 11. Process Success Measures

Potential measures for evaluating the proposed solution could include:

* User engagement with recommended music
* Interaction with recommended content
* Discovery of previously unfamiliar music
* User satisfaction with recommendation relevance
* Creator visibility and engagement
* Completion of key discovery activities

These measures would require appropriate definitions, data sources and acceptance thresholds before being used as formal business or product metrics.
