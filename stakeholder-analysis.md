# Stakeholder Analysis

## Stakeholder Overview

The project identified several stakeholder groups involved in the music discovery and recommendation system.

The main stakeholders are:

- Listeners
- Artists
- Record labels
- Product managers

Each stakeholder has different needs, expectations and interests in the proposed solution.

## Stakeholder Needs

### Listeners

Listeners need an easy and personalised way to discover music that matches their interests and listening preferences.

**Key needs:**
- Personalised music recommendations
- Easy music discovery
- Relevant and engaging recommendations
- A simple user experience

### Artists

Artists need greater visibility and opportunities for their music to be discovered by relevant listeners.

**Key needs:**
- Increased music visibility
- Opportunities to reach new listeners
- Fairer opportunities for discovery

### Record Labels

Record labels have an interest in increasing the visibility and reach of music within the platform.

**Key needs:**
- Increased exposure for music content
- Improved audience reach
- Support for music discovery

### Product Managers

Product managers are responsible for ensuring that the proposed solution addresses both business and user needs.

**Key needs:**
- Clear business requirements
- User-focused product decisions
- Requirements that can be translated into system functionality
- Alignment between stakeholder needs and the proposed solution

## Stakeholder Influence and Interest

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Key Concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listeners | High | High | Personalised and relevant recommendations |
| Artists | High | Medium | Music visibility and discovery |
| Record labels | High | Medium | Audience reach and content visibility |
| Product managers | High | High | Business value and user needs |

## Stakeholder Engagement

Stakeholder needs should be considered throughout the requirements analysis and solution design process.

The analysis should ensure that user needs are balanced with business objectives and the requirements of music creators and other stakeholders.

## Summary

The stakeholder analysis provides a clear view of the different groups affected by the proposed music discovery and recommendation system. Understanding their needs helps ensure that the proposed solution addresses both user and business requirements.
